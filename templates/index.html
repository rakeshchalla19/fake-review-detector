The dataset

Fake Reviews Dataset by Joni Salminen, Chandrashekhar Kandpal, Ahmed Mohamed Kamel, Soon-gyo Jung, and Bernard J. Jansen.

OR: original product reviews, presumed human and authentic, not independently verified.
CG: computer-generated reviews produced with GPT-2.

These labels do not represent verified human deception or platform filtering. AI authorship and false statements are different concepts.

The methodology

  1. Normalize for duplicate detection; remove exact duplicates and collapse detected near-duplicate groups before splitting.
  2. Use separate stratified train / validation / test partitions (60 / 20 / 20).
  3. Fit word unigram/bigram TF-IDF on training data only.
  4. Compare majority baseline, Logistic Regression, and Linear SVM; select by validation F1.
  5. Evaluate once on held-out test data and save the fitted pipeline.

MinHash candidate search is approximate. Product IDs are unavailable; product-disjoint performance is not established.

Limits that matter

This dataset reflects older GPT-2 text and a limited set of product categories. Performance on modern language models, unfamiliar categories, human-written deception, and other languages is unverified.

A positive score is not proof of a fake review. Sentiment, writing style, and review length may act as shortcuts. No calibrated probabilities are shown.

Language detection is approximate and can reject valid short or mixed-language English reviews. Similarity indicates shared wording, not misconduct.

Privacy & platform access

Submitted reviews are processed in server memory and never saved to a database, logs, or browser storage by this application. The page keeps results in memory until you refresh or clear them. Optional photos are previewed in your browser only; they are never sent to the server and are not analyzed by the text model.

Hosting infrastructure may record ordinary request metadata. Avoid sensitive personal information.

There is no product-URL retrieval or scraping. Upload a permitted CSV or paste review text. No Amazon or other marketplace API integration is claimed.
