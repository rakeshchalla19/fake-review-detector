MODEL CARD / DATA NOTES

What the score means.

An unsupervised baseline for review-pattern screening.

Training data

Amazon Reviews ’23, collected by McAuley Lab. The deployed model is trained on a deterministic sample from the All_Beauty category.

The corpus includes review text, rating, product ID, reviewer ID, timestamp, verified-purchase status, and helpful votes. It does not label reviews fake or genuine.

Amazon Reviews ’23 project ↗

Raw review text is not included in this repository. The training script downloads it when run.

Method

  1. Represent review text with word and phrase features.
  2. Add rating, verification, helpfulness, length, and within-upload history counts when supplied.
  3. Use an Isolation Forest to rank statistically unusual reviews.
  4. Use a 5% shortlist setting for the baseline; this is not fake-review prevalence.

No supervised fraud accuracy or probability is claimed.

Limits

An unusual pattern can come from an uncommon writing style, rating, or metadata combination. It does not show that a reviewer lied. A normal-looking review can still be deceptive.

Training uses one category and reviews collected through September 2023. Generalization to every product category and current review manipulation has not been validated.

Privacy & access

Submitted reviews and reviewer IDs are processed in memory and are not saved by this app. Reviewer/product repetition counts are calculated only within the current CSV upload.

Amazon product links are not fetched. Upload a CSV you are allowed to analyze. Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary request metadata.
